# B0079 · Delivering bad news to stakeholders

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Integrity | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you had to deliver bad news to stakeholders.

## Answer

- Situation: two weeks before launch, evaluation showed the agent at 70% accuracy against the 90% that had been promised.
- Action: told stakeholders immediately with the facts, the options (delay; launch a narrower scope with human review; invest in better data), your recommendation and what you were already doing about it. No sugar-coating.
- Result: they chose the narrower launch with review, and trust was maintained.
- Principle: bad news travels early, with a plan attached.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you deliver bad news to a senior executive?
- What if the bad news was your own fault?
