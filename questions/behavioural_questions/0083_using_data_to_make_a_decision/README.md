# B0083 · Using data to make a decision

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - judgment | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you used data to make or change a decision.

## Answer

- Situation: the team was debating moving to a larger model to fix answer quality.
- Action: analysed 500 failed conversations and found 60% were retrieval misses, not reasoning failures. You then compared retrieval fixes (hybrid search plus a reranker) with the bigger model on the evaluation set.
- Result: the retrieval fix improved answer accuracy by 18 points at a fifth of the cost of the model upgrade, and the decision changed.

Show hypothesis-driven analysis, representative sampling and clear communication of the result.

## Likely follow-ups

- How did you make sure the sample was representative?
- What do you do when the data and your intuition disagree?
