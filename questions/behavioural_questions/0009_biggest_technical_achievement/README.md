# B0009 · Biggest technical achievement

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|

## Question

What is your biggest technical achievement?

## Answer

Use STAR and spend most of the time on Action and Result:

- Situation/Task: context and why it mattered (users, risk, scale).
- Action: three or four decisions you personally made, with the alternatives. Show depth, for example moving from polling to SSE streaming, adding idempotency keys to tool calls, building an evaluation harness.
- Result: numbers such as latency, cost, adoption, incident reduction or hours saved.
- Reflection: what you learned and what you would improve.

Pick something relevant to the JD (LLM platform, agents, reliability). Avoid a team achievement where your part is unclear.

## Likely follow-ups

- What was the hardest trade-off?
- Who disagreed with you, and how was it resolved?
- How did you measure the result?
