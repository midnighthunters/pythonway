# B0012 · Where do you see yourself in five years

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Motivation | Easy |

## Question

Where do you see yourself in three to five years?

## Answer

- Growth along the engineering track: from Senior Associate towards Lead Software Engineer level, owning a platform area such as the agent runtime, tool gateway or evaluation framework.
- Depth: become a go-to engineer for agent reliability and security; contribute to internal standards or to open protocols such as MCP and A2A.
- Impact: capabilities you built are used across several lines of business.
- Keep it realistic and team-oriented; avoid "managing a team within a year".

## Likely follow-ups

- What skills do you need to build to get there?
- How would you know you are on track?
