# B0078 · Challenging the status quo

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |

## Question

Tell me about a time you challenged the status quo.

## Answer

- Situation: the team tested prompts by hand, because "that's how we've always done it".
- Action: gathered evidence (regressions that had shipped), proposed evaluation-driven development, built a lightweight harness, ran it alongside the old process to prove value, took concerns (false positives) seriously and iterated.
- Result: it became the standard and regressions dropped.

Tie it to courage and integrity: you challenged respectfully and with data.

## Likely follow-ups

- What resistance did you face?
- When should you not challenge the status quo?
