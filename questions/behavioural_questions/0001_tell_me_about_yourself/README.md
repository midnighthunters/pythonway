## Question

Tell me about yourself and why you are a fit for LLM Suite Engineering.

## Answer

Aim for about 90 seconds using present → past → future:

- Present: "I'm a backend/AI engineer with N years building Python services. For the last X years I've shipped LLM-powered and agentic features to production: LangGraph agents behind FastAPI services on Azure and AWS."
- Past: two highlights with numbers, e.g. "built a RAG assistant used by 3,000 analysts and cut p95 latency from 9s to 3s", "added evaluation gates to CI that caught a regression before a model upgrade".
- Future: "LLM Suite is one of the largest enterprise GenAI platforms. I want to help make agentic capabilities (MCP, A2A, orchestration) production-grade in a regulated environment."

Tips: echo JD language (secure, reliable, production-ready, Azure OpenAI/Bedrock, FastAPI, LangGraph), don't recite your CV, and end on a hook the interviewer will want to ask about.

## Likely follow-ups

- Which of those projects are you most proud of, and why?
- What was your specific contribution versus the team's?
- Why are you looking now?
