# B0013 · What are your strengths

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Self-awareness | Easy |

## Question

What are your main strengths?

## Answer

Pick two or three strengths that matter for this role and prove each with evidence:

- Production mindset: "I design for failure with timeouts, retries and idempotency; my last service held 99.95% availability over a year."
- Learning speed: "I shipped an MCP integration two weeks after picking up the spec."
- Collaboration: "I ask for design reviews early; one review caught a data-residency issue before build."

Avoid lists of adjectives. One short story per strength is enough.

## Likely follow-ups

- Which of your strengths causes problems when overused?
- How would your teammates describe you?
