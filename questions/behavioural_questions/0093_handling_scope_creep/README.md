# B0093 · Handling scope creep

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |

## Question

How do you handle scope creep during a project?

## Answer

- Anchor on the goals, success metrics and non-goals written down at the start.
- When new asks arrive: acknowledge them, assess the impact on time and risk, and present options (swap something out, move the date, defer to a later phase).
- Make the decision explicit with the product owner, then update and communicate the plan.
- Keep a backlog of deferred ideas so stakeholders feel heard.
- In AI projects, creep often looks like "just add one more tool or data source". Check the impact on quality as well as effort.
