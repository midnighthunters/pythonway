# B0034 · Worked with a difficult teammate

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - collaboration | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you worked with a difficult teammate.

## Answer

- Situation: describe concrete behaviour (dismissive reviews, missed handoffs), not personality labels.
- Action: sought to understand in a 1:1 (what pressures they had), aligned on shared goals, agreed working norms (review SLAs, clear ownership in tickets), kept discussions factual, and involved the manager only after trying directly.
- Result: collaboration improved and the project delivered; say what you learned about empathy and clarity.
- Avoid blame. Interviewers are listening for patience and professionalism.

## Likely follow-ups

- What did you learn about yourself?
- What would you have done if the behaviour didn't change?
