# B0046 · Saying no to a stakeholder

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - communication | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you had to say no to a stakeholder.

## Answer

- Situation: a request to wire an unapproved external API into an agent to hit a demo date.
- Action: acknowledged the goal, explained why not (data policy, security review needed), offered alternatives (an approved internal model, a mocked demo with synthetic data, the expedited review route) and gave a realistic timeline.
- Result: the demo went ahead on the mock, and the real integration was approved later.
- Principles: "No, but here is what we can do", transparency, and protecting the relationship.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you handle pressure from someone senior?
- When is "yes" the right answer despite the risk?
