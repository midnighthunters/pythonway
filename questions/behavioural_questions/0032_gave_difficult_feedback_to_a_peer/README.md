# B0032 · Gave difficult feedback to a peer

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - feedback | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you had to give difficult feedback to a colleague.

## Answer

- Situation: e.g. a peer repeatedly merged changes without tests, which caused incidents.
- Action: a private conversation soon after a specific instance, using Situation–Behaviour–Impact: "In yesterday's deploy, the retry change went in without tests, and it caused the duplicate emails." Ask for their view (perhaps deadline pressure) and agree the fix together (pairing on tests, a CI coverage gate).
- Result: the behaviour changed, incidents dropped, the relationship stayed intact.
- Principles: specific, timely, private, about behaviour rather than the person, with a collaborative next step.

## Likely follow-ups

- What if they had reacted defensively?
- When would you involve a manager?
