# B0082 · Ethically questionable AI use case

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Responsible AI | Hard |

## Question

You are asked to build an AI feature that feels ethically questionable, for example analysing employee chats to score productivity. How do you handle it?

## Answer

- Clarify purpose and scope: what decision it supports, who is affected, and whether it is proportionate.
- Raise concerns early with the product owner and the right partners (HR, legal, privacy, compliance, AI governance). Monitoring employees has legal implications (UK GDPR lawful basis, DPIA, transparency, works councils in parts of the EU) and trust implications.
- Propose alternatives: aggregated or anonymised insights, opt-in tools, explicit consent, or helping employees rather than scoring them.
- If the firm proceeds lawfully with governance approval, build in safeguards: transparency, minimisation, human review, fairness testing.
- If you believe it is unlawful or unethical, escalate under the code of conduct; you can decline to take part.

## Likely follow-ups

- Where do you draw your personal line?
- Which UK regulations apply?
