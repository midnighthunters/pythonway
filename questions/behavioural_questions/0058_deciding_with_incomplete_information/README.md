# B0058 · Deciding with incomplete information

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - judgment | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with incomplete information.

## Answer

- Situation: choosing a vector store or model provider before full benchmarks were available, with a deadline approaching.
- Action: identified what mattered (latency, filtering, operational burden, security approval), gathered quick evidence (a one-day spike on representative data), judged reversibility (two-way doors decide fast; one-way doors slow down), chose with written assumptions and an exit plan (an abstraction layer), and set checkpoints to revisit.
- Result: shipped on time and revisited at month three with real metrics.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you tell a one-way door from a two-way door?
- What happens if your key assumption proves wrong?
