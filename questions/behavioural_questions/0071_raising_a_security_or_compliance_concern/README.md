# B0071 · Raising a security or compliance concern

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Integrity | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you raised a security or compliance concern.

## Answer

- Situation: you noticed an agent's logs captured full prompts, including client names and account numbers, retained indefinitely in a widely accessible index.
- Action: verified it, informed the owner and the security team through the proper channel, proposed a fix (redact PII before logging, restrict access, apply a retention policy), helped implement it, and checked whether it needed to be reported as an incident.
- Result: fixed within a sprint, and redaction became a shared library.

Show courage, process and collaboration, not blame.

## Likely follow-ups

- What if the team lead had dismissed the concern?
- How do you decide whether something counts as an incident?
