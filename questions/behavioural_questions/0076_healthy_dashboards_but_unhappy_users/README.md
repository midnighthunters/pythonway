# B0076 · Healthy dashboards but unhappy users

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Product thinking | Medium |

## Question

Your AI feature's dashboards look healthy, but users are complaining. What do you do?

## Answer

- Take complaints seriously: the metrics may measure the wrong thing (availability is not usefulness).
- Gather specifics: sample complaints, pull the session traces, talk to five to ten users.
- Segment the metrics by user group, document type, query type and time of day. Averages hide failure pockets such as long documents or one business unit.
- Check quality signals: grounding, citation accuracy, refusal rate, the p99 latency tail, streams that stall.
- Add the missing metrics: task success, re-ask rate, copy rate, abandonment, thumbs-down reasons.
- Close the loop: add the failing cases to the evaluation set and tell users what changed.

## Likely follow-ups

- Which implicit signals indicate dissatisfaction?
- How do you prevent this blind spot in future?
