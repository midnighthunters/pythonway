# B0038 · Influencing without authority

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - influence | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you influenced a decision without formal authority.

## Answer

- Situation: e.g. you wanted teams to run an evaluation harness before shipping prompt changes.
- Action: built a small prototype, showed a real regression it would have caught, quantified the cost of that incident, made adoption cheap (template repo, CI step), found an early-adopter team and shared their results.
- Result: several teams adopted it and it became part of the release checklist.
- Principles: evidence, empathy for other teams' priorities, and lowering the cost of saying yes.

## Likely follow-ups

- Who resisted, and why?
- What would you have done if nobody adopted it?
