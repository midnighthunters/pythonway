## Question

What is different about building GenAI in a global bank compared with a startup?

## Answer

- Risk and controls: model risk management, data classification, security, legal and compliance approvals, and an audit trail for prompts, outputs and actions.
- Data: need-to-know entitlements, data residency, information barriers (for example between public-side and private-side teams), retention and eDiscovery.
- Reliability: people depend on it for real work, so operational resilience and change management matter.
- Scale and heterogeneity: many lines of business, legacy systems, identity and entitlement platforms to integrate.
- Vendor risk: model providers are critical suppliers, which pushes towards multi-provider designs and exit plans.
- Upside: enormous leverage, since a small gain times hundreds of thousands of users is large.

Frame constraints as design inputs, not blockers.
