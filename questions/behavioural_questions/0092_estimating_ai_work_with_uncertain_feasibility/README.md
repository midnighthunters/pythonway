# B0092 · Estimating AI work with uncertain feasibility

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Delivery | Medium |

## Question

How do you estimate and plan an AI feature when you don't yet know whether the model can do the task well enough?

## Answer

- Separate feasibility from delivery: a timeboxed spike (one or two weeks) that builds an evaluation set and a quick baseline (prompting, RAG) against explicit go/no-go criteria.
- Estimate the engineering (APIs, integration, security, UI) conventionally, and plan the quality work as iterations with checkpoints.
- Communicate ranges and assumptions, and show the quality curve after each iteration.
- Plan fallbacks: narrower scope, human review, or a non-AI alternative.
- Re-estimate at every checkpoint.

## Likely follow-ups

- What would your go/no-go criteria look like?
- How do you communicate uncertainty to executives?
