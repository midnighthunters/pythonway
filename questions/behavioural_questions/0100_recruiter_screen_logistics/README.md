# B0100 · Recruiter screen logistics

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Recruiter screen | Easy |

## Question

What is your notice period, what are your compensation expectations, and do you have the right to work in the UK? How do you feel about the office attendance expectations?

## Answer

- Notice period: state it clearly and say whether it is negotiable.
- Compensation: research the London band for Software Engineer III / Senior Associate (base plus bonus) and give a range based on market data and total compensation. It is fine to ask the recruiter for the band first.
- Right to work: answer factually (citizenship, settled status, or visa type and whether sponsorship is needed).
- Office attendance: JPMorganChase publicly moved most hybrid employees back to five days a week in the office from 2025. Confirm the current expectation for this team and that it works for you.
- Ask about the process: stages, timeline, interviewers, and what to prepare (HackerRank, system design, behavioral).

## Likely follow-ups

- Which other processes are you in, and at what stage?
- When could you start?
