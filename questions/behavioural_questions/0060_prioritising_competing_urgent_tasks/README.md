# B0060 · Prioritising competing urgent tasks

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - prioritization | Easy |

## Question

How do you prioritise when several urgent tasks land at once?

## Answer

- Triage by impact and urgency: production incidents and security issues first, then commitments others depend on, then the rest.
- Clarify with stakeholders what is truly urgent and what delay costs.
- Make trade-offs visible: tell affected people what slips and agree it with your manager.
- Protect focus time, break work down, and share or delegate where you can.

Example: an incident, a feature deadline and a review queue at once. You handled the incident, negotiated a one-day slip on the feature, and batched the reviews.
