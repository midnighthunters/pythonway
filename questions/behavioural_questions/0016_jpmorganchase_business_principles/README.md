## Question

What are JPMorganChase's Business Principles, and how would they show up in your engineering work?

## Answer

The firm describes four pillars in "How We Do Business":

- Exceptional client service: for an internal platform the clients are employees and application teams, so clear docs, usable SDKs, dependable SLAs and fast response to feedback.
- Operational excellence: tested code, observability, controlled releases, runbooks, blameless postmortems, cost discipline.
- A commitment to integrity, fairness and responsibility: protecting data, reporting incidents and evaluation results honestly, responsible AI, following controls under deadline pressure.
- A great team and winning culture: share credit, give and receive feedback, mentor, include people.

Prepare one short story per pillar; behavioral questions are often mapped to them.

## Likely follow-ups

- Which principle is hardest to live by under deadline pressure?
- Give an example of operational excellence from your own work.
