## Question

How does regulation affect the way you ship AI features in a bank?

## Answer

- Model risk management: GenAI uses may need inventory, risk tiering, validation and monitoring (US SR 11-7; UK PRA SS1/23 principles).
- Data protection: UK/EU GDPR (lawful basis, minimisation, DPIAs), client confidentiality, cross-border transfer rules.
- Conduct and customer outcomes: e.g. the FCA Consumer Duty if outputs affect customers.
- Accountability: named owners (UK Senior Managers and Certification Regime), approvals, audit trail.
- EU AI Act for in-scope uses; for example credit scoring is classed as high-risk.
- Operational resilience and third-party risk: critical suppliers, exit plans.

Engineering translation: audit logging, evaluation evidence, access controls, human oversight, documentation and change control. You are not the lawyer; know when to involve risk and compliance.

## Likely follow-ups

- What evidence would a model validator ask you for?
- How does this change your release process?
