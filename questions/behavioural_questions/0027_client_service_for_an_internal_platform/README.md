# B0027 · Client service for an internal platform

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|

## Question

How does "exceptional client service" apply when your clients are internal teams and employees?

## Answer

- Know your clients: application teams integrating through APIs and SDKs, and end users of LLM Suite. Talk to them and watch usage data.
- Make it easy: clear docs, quick-start samples, stable versioned APIs, useful error messages, sandbox environments.
- Be dependable: published SLOs, a status page, proactive incident communication, deprecation notices with migration guides.
- Be responsive: support triage SLAs, office hours, feedback flowing into the roadmap.
- Say no well: explain the constraint (risk, capacity) and offer an alternative.

Example: "After repeated 'why am I getting 429s?' tickets, I added a self-service quota dashboard; those tickets fell by 70%."

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you prioritise between two client teams' requests?
- How do you communicate a breaking change?
