# B0011 · Why are you leaving your current role

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Recruiter screen | Easy |

## Question

Why are you looking to leave your current role?

## Answer

Keep it brief, positive and forward-looking:

- Pull, not push: "I want to work on agentic AI at enterprise scale in a regulated environment, and LLM Suite is one of the few places doing that at this scale."
- Growth: more design exposure with senior engineers, platform work with a larger blast radius.
- Never criticise your current employer. If there is a real reason (reorganisation, cancelled project), state it neutrally in one sentence and move on.

## Likely follow-ups

- What would your current manager say about you?
- What would make you stay where you are?
