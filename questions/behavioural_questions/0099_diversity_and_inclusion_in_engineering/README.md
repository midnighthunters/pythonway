# B0099 · Diversity and inclusion in engineering

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Culture | Easy |

## Question

What does diversity and inclusion mean to you in an engineering team?

## Answer

- Diverse teams build better products, especially AI tools used by hundreds of thousands of very different employees. Different perspectives catch blind spots in bias, accessibility and language.
- Inclusive habits: make room for quieter voices, accept written async input, rotate presenters, review code fairly, mentor, make decision processes clear.
- In the product: accessible UIs (WCAG), screen-reader-friendly output, multilingual support, fairness testing.
- Give one example of something you did, such as a knowledge-sharing series or better onboarding for career changers.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you make sure every voice is heard in design reviews?
- How does inclusion relate to AI fairness?
