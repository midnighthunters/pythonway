# B0028 · Stakeholder request that conflicts with policy

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Integrity | Medium |

## Question

A business stakeholder wants a feature that conflicts with a risk or data policy. How do you handle it?

## Answer

- Understand the underlying need: what outcome they want and why it is urgent.
- Clarify the policy: read it and ask the control owner (security, data office, compliance) exactly what is prohibited and why.
- Offer compliant alternatives: an approved internal model instead of an external API, redaction or pseudonymisation, a sandbox with synthetic data, human approval, narrower scope.
- If it is still blocked, escalate transparently with options and risks written down, and let the accountable owners decide (possibly a formal risk acceptance).
- Never quietly build the non-compliant path.

Close with the outcome of a real example.

## Likely follow-ups

- What if the stakeholder is very senior?
- What is a risk acceptance, and who signs it?
