## Question

Why do you want to work at JPMorganChase?

## Answer

Layer three reasons and make them specific:

- Scale and impact: LLM Suite went from zero to about 200,000 onboarded users within eight months of its summer 2024 launch, and it serves corporate functions such as Finance, Treasury, Risk, Compliance, Legal and HR. Small improvements multiply across a very large workforce.
- Commitment: the firm invests heavily in technology (publicly reported at around $18B a year) and treats AI as strategic. LLM Suite won American Banker's 2025 Innovation of the Year grand prize.
- Engineering maturity: you want to learn to ship AI that is secure, auditable and reliable in a regulated environment, which is harder and more valuable than demos.
- Culture fit: connect one Business Principle (client service, operational excellence, integrity, team) to a real example of how you work.

Avoid generic "prestigious bank" answers; tie everything back to this role and your story.

## Likely follow-ups

- What do you know about our Business Principles?
- Which other companies are you speaking to, and why us?
- What would make you leave after a year?
