| Section | Topic | Difficulty |

## Question

Tell me about a time you helped a colleague grow.

## Answer

- Situation: a junior engineer new to async Python and LangGraph.
- Action: paired on their first feature, shared a short reading list, gave them ownership of a well-scoped component with a safety net, explained the "why" in code reviews, gradually reduced support, and encouraged them to present at the team demo.
- Result: they delivered the next feature independently, and you turned the experience into an onboarding guide.
- Signal for this level: you multiply the team, not just your own output.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you adapt mentoring to different people?
- What did you learn from mentoring?
