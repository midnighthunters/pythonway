# B0006 · Why Corporate Technology in London

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Motivation | Easy |

## Question

This role sits in Corporate Technology in London. Why does that appeal to you?

## Answer

- Corporate Technology builds for the firm's corporate functions: Global Finance, Corporate Treasury, Risk Management, HR, Compliance, Legal and the Corporate Administrative Office. That work is document-heavy and controls-heavy, which suits GenAI well.
- An internal platform gives a short feedback loop with real users and measurable productivity outcomes.
- London is a major JPMorganChase hub with global teams. Show that you are comfortable collaborating across New York, India and European time zones (overlap hours, written design docs, async updates).
- Practical points to confirm: hybrid expectations and right to work.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you work effectively with colleagues in other time zones?
- Which corporate-function use case interests you most?
