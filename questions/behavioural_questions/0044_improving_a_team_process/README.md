# B0044 · Improving a team process

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - ownership | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you improved a team process.

## Answer

- Situation: e.g. pull requests waiting three days for review, or flaky CI.
- Action: measured the baseline, proposed small changes (PR size guidance, a review rotation, CODEOWNERS, CI caching, quarantining flaky tests), piloted them for two sprints and collected feedback.
- Result: review time from three days to six hours, CI from 25 to 9 minutes, and the team kept the changes.

Show that the change was data-driven and collaborative rather than imposed.

## Likely follow-ups

- How did you get buy-in?
- What didn't work?
