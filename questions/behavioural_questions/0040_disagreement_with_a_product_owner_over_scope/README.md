# B0040 · Disagreement with a product owner over scope

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|

## Question

Tell me about a time you disagreed with a product owner about scope or priorities.

## Answer

- Situation: the product owner wanted three extra tools added to an agent before launch, but evaluation coverage and reliability were not ready.
- Action: framed it around the shared goal (a successful launch), quantified the risk (tool-selection accuracy fell from 92% to 78% on the evaluation set with the extra tools), proposed a phased scope (launch with the core tools, add the rest behind flags once evaluated), and wrote the trade-off down.
- Result: the phased plan was agreed, launch was on time, and the extra tools shipped two sprints later with good metrics.

## Likely follow-ups

- What if the product owner had insisted?
- How do you say no without damaging the relationship?
