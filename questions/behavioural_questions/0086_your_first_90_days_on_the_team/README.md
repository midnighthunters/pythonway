## Question

If you joined the LLM Suite Engineering team, what would you do in your first 90 days?

## Answer

- Days 1–30, learn: set up the environment and ship a small fix; understand the architecture (gateway, orchestration, model adapters, tools, data), SLOs, incidents and controls; meet stakeholders; read ADRs and postmortems.
- Days 31–60, contribute: own a scoped feature end to end, shadow on-call, and propose one data-backed improvement (evaluation coverage, a latency hotspot).
- Days 61–90, own: deliver a meaningful capability (for example an MCP tool integration or an A2A adapter) with tests, docs and dashboards; share what you learned; agree growth goals with your manager.

Emphasise listening before changing things.

## Likely follow-ups

- What would you want to know on day one?
- How would you measure your own success after 90 days?
