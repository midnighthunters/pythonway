# B0047 · Taking ownership outside your role

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - ownership | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you took responsibility for something outside your formal role.

## Answer

- Situation: e.g. nobody owned expiring TLS certificates, an orphaned batch job, or alerting on a shared dependency.
- Action: raised it, proposed an owner, removed the immediate risk (automated renewal, new alerts), documented it and handed it over cleanly.
- Result: an outage avoided, and the practice adopted by other services.
- Show initiative without overstepping: you worked with the actual owners rather than around them.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you avoid spreading yourself too thin?
- What if the owning team hadn't welcomed your help?
