# B0055 · Asked to skip a required control

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Integrity | Medium |

## Question

What would you do if a manager asked you to skip a required control, such as a security review or tests, to meet a deadline?

## Answer

The expected answer is direct: you do not silently skip it.

- Clarify the constraint and state the risk plainly: what could go wrong and who is exposed.
- Propose alternatives: reduce scope, ship behind a flag to a limited pilot, request an expedited review, run the critical subset of tests, or move the date.
- If pressure continues, escalate through proper channels (the control owner or risk partner) and document it. Only an accountable owner can grant a formal exception.
- Never falsify evidence or bypass the control.

## Likely follow-ups

- What if everyone else on the team was doing it?
- When is an exception legitimate?
