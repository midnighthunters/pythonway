# B0045 · Disagree and commit

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - collaboration | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you committed to a team decision you initially disagreed with.

## Answer

- Situation: the team chose an approach you thought was suboptimal, e.g. a vector database you felt was over-engineered for the load.
- Action: raised your concerns with data in the decision forum; once the decision was made, committed fully, implemented it well, documented the risks and added metrics to test the assumptions.
- Result: either it worked and you learned something, or the data later justified a revision, which you raised constructively.
- The signal: no quiet resistance and no "I told you so".

Note the exception: if a decision is unsafe or unethical, you escalate rather than commit.

## Likely follow-ups

- When would you revisit a decision?
- Where is the line between disagreeing and escalating?
