# B0098 · On-call and sustainable pace

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |

## Question

How do you approach on-call, and how do you keep a sustainable pace?

## Answer

- On-call: know the runbooks, test your access before the shift, acknowledge quickly, follow the incident process, escalate early, write clear handoff notes.
- Improve the rotation: fix noisy alerts, automate toil, track postmortem actions and pages per shift.
- Sustainability: protect focus time, make realistic commitments, say so when overloaded, rotate responsibilities, take recovery time after heavy incidents.
