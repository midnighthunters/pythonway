# B0070 · Going above and beyond for a user

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - client service | Easy |

## Question

Tell me about a time you went above and beyond for a client or user.

## Answer

- Situation: a Legal team couldn't use the document Q&A tool because their scanned PDFs weren't parsed.
- Action: went beyond the ticket. You analysed their document types, added an OCR and layout-parsing step, validated it on their sample set, trained their power users and set up a feedback channel.
- Result: adoption across the team, and the ingestion step became a reusable capability.
- Balance: you did it within agreed priorities, with your manager's support.

## Likely follow-ups

- How did you balance this with your other commitments?
- How did you measure the client's satisfaction?
