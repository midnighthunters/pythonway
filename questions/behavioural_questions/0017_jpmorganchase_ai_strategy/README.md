## Question

How would you describe JPMorganChase's AI strategy from what is publicly known?

## Answer

Publicly reported themes:

- Firmwide adoption: LLM Suite as the employee GenAI hub, used by a large share of staff.
- Secure, model-agnostic platform: avoid lock-in to a single provider and keep data in controlled environments.
- From assistants to agents: connect internal data and workflows so AI completes multi-step work (drafting, research, operations).
- Heavy investment in data, AI talent and infrastructure, with firmwide AI adoption driven by the Chief Data & Analytics Office.
- Value focus: productivity, client experience and risk management (fraud, compliance).

Then show the engineering implications: platform reliability, cost management, controls, evaluation and change management for users.

## Likely follow-ups

- What risks come with firmwide AI adoption?
- How would you measure the value delivered?
