# B0049 · Putting the team ahead of your own work

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - teamwork | Easy |

## Question

Tell me about a time you helped the team succeed at the expense of your own individual work.

## Answer

- Situation: a release was at risk because a teammate's component was behind.
- Action: paused your lower-priority feature, paired with them, wrote tests, took part of the work, and kept the manager informed of the trade-off.
- Result: the release shipped on time; your feature slipped one sprint with stakeholder agreement.

This shows the "great team and winning culture" principle plus clear communication about trade-offs.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you keep your own contribution visible?
- When would you not do this?
