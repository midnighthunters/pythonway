# B0036 · Explaining technical issues to non-technical people

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - communication | Easy |

## Question

Tell me about a time you explained a complex technical issue to a non-technical stakeholder.

## Answer

- Situation: e.g. a Compliance lead asked why the assistant had "made up" a regulation citation.
- Action: explained hallucination with an analogy (very fluent autocomplete that predicts plausible text), what the team does about it (grounding in approved documents, citations, "I don't know" behaviour, evaluation), the residual risk, and what users should do (check citations). Used a one-page visual, no jargon, and confirmed understanding by asking which decision they needed to make.
- Result: they approved a pilot with citations and a review step.
- Tip: lead with the impact and the decision needed, not the mechanics.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you know they really understood?
- How would you explain RAG in one sentence?
