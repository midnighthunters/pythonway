# B0056 · Missed a deadline or goal

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - accountability | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a goal or deadline you missed. What happened next?

## Answer

Spend most of the answer on what you changed.

- Situation: an agent integration slipped two weeks because the entitlement integration was underestimated.
- Action: flagged the slip as soon as you knew, not at the deadline; re-planned with options (reduced scope vs a new date); delivered a partial capability; ran a retrospective.
- Changes you made: spikes on unknown dependencies before committing dates, estimates as ranges, explicit dependency tracking, earlier integration tests.
- Result: the next project landed on its date.

## Likely follow-ups

- When did you first realise you would miss it?
- How did stakeholders react?
