# B0057 · Tell me about a failure

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - accountability | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you failed.

## Answer

- Choose a real failure with meaningful stakes: not trivial, and not misconduct.
- Own it without excuses: "I chose X, and it was wrong because…"
- Explain how you recovered and limited the impact.
- Show what you learned and the evidence you have applied it since.

Example: you launched a feature without load-testing long-lived streaming connections; the gateway hit its connection limit on day one and you rolled back. You now include connection-level capacity tests and staged rollouts in every launch plan.

## Likely follow-ups

- What would you do differently?
- How did your team respond?
