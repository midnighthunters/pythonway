# B0065 · Pushing back on an unrealistic estimate

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - communication | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you pushed back on an unrealistic deadline or estimate.

## Answer

- Situation: asked to deliver a multi-agent workflow, including security review, in three weeks.
- Action: broke down the work, identified the unknowns (review lead time, integrations), gave a range with assumptions, and offered options: a single agent with human review by week three, full scope by week seven. You also flagged the risk of cutting testing.
- Result: stakeholders chose the phased option, you delivered as promised, and you built credibility.

## Likely follow-ups

- What if the date genuinely could not move?
- How do you estimate AI work when quality is uncertain?
