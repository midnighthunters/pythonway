# B0042 · Asking for help early

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - teamwork | Easy |

## Question

Tell me about a time you asked for help. How do you decide when to ask?

## Answer

- Rule of thumb: timebox it (30–60 minutes without progress, or sooner if it touches another team's domain or security), then ask with context: what you tried, your hypotheses, the logs.
- Example: intermittent 502s on SSE streams through the gateway. After an hour you asked the platform team, who pointed at the load balancer idle timeout; heartbeat events fixed it.
- The signal: humility, respect for other people's time, and speed.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you avoid asking too often?
- How do you make it easy for others to ask you for help?
