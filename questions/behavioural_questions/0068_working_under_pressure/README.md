# B0068 · Working under pressure

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - resilience | Easy |

## Question

Tell me about a time you had to work under significant pressure.

## Answer

- Situation: a firmwide launch in 48 hours and a critical bug in streaming reconnects.
- Action: stayed calm, prioritised the critical path and deferred nice-to-haves, gave regular status updates, split the work across the team, kept testing discipline (no untested hotfix) and used a feature flag as a safety net.
- Result: launched on time with the bug fixed and covered by tests.

Show a sustainable approach rather than heroics, and mention what you changed to avoid the crunch next time.

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you avoid burnout?
- What would you change to avoid that pressure in future?
