## Question

Why this team? Why build on LLM Suite rather than an AI product somewhere else?

## Answer

- The problems are hard in the right ways: multi-model routing, entitlements, guardrails, reliability and cost for hundreds of thousands of users.
- The JD targets the frontier: operationalising agent-to-agent communication, the Model Context Protocol, agentic orchestration, AI skills and personal assistants, moving from chat to agents that complete multi-step work.
- Platform leverage: one capability, such as a governed MCP tool gateway, unlocks hundreds of use cases across the firm.
- Your fit: map two or three concrete experiences (a LangGraph agent in production, FastAPI microservices on Azure/AWS, Azure OpenAI or Bedrock integrations).
- Growth: the JD emphasises design discussions with senior engineers and applying feedback, which is how you like to learn.

## Likely follow-ups

- Which JD requirement is your strongest, and which is your weakest?
- What agentic capability would you want to ship first?
- How would you explain LLM Suite to a new joiner?
