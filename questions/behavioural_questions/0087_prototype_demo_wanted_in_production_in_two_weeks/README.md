# B0087 · Prototype demo wanted in production in two weeks

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Scenario | Hard |

## Question

A proof-of-concept agent impressed leadership, and they want it in production in two weeks. What do you do?

## Answer

- Welcome the enthusiasm and make the gap visible: the prototype lacks auth and entitlements, evaluation, error handling, security review, monitoring, runbooks, load testing and data approvals.
- Offer a phased plan:
  - Weeks 1–2: a limited pilot (say 50 users) on the paved-road platform (gateway, guardrails, logging), read-only or draft-only actions, human review, a kill switch, basic evaluations and dashboards.
  - Following weeks: security and model-risk review, scale testing, wider rollout against agreed criteria.
- Quantify the risks of skipping steps (data leakage, wrong actions, reputational damage).
- Agree success criteria for each phase with leadership.

## Likely follow-ups

- What is the minimum you would not compromise on?
- How do you keep momentum through the reviews?
