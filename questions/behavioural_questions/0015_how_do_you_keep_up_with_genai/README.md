## Question

GenAI changes monthly. How do you keep up, and how do you decide what to adopt?

## Answer

- Primary sources first: provider changelogs (Azure OpenAI in Microsoft Foundry, Amazon Bedrock), specification revisions (MCP 2026-07-28, A2A v1.0), LangChain/LangGraph release notes, selected papers.
- Hands-on spikes: e.g. migrate a small MCP server to the stateless revision, or compare two models on your own evaluation set.
- Community: engineering blogs, conference talks, internal guilds.
- Filter: "Does this change our architecture, cost or risk?" Adopt through an evaluated experiment, not hype.

Give one recent example of something you learned and applied at work.

## Likely follow-ups

- What is the most important change in the last six months?
- How do you decide whether to adopt a new framework?
