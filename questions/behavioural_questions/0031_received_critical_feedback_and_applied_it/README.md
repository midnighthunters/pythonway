# B0031 · Received critical feedback and applied it

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - feedback | Medium |

## Question

Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. What did you do with it?

## Answer

The JD explicitly asks for someone who seeks and applies feedback, so make this story strong.

- Situation: e.g. reviewers said your pull requests were too large to review, or a design review found your agent had no failure handling.
- Task: improve without being defensive.
- Action: thanked the reviewer, asked for concrete examples, made a specific change (PRs under ~400 lines, a design note before coding, a failure-mode checklist) and asked for follow-up feedback a few weeks later.
- Result: measurable change (review turnaround from three days to one, fewer post-merge bugs) and a better working relationship.
- Lesson: you now ask for feedback at the design stage.

## Likely follow-ups

- How did you feel when you received it?
- When did you last ask for feedback proactively?
