# B0014 · What is your biggest weakness

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Self-awareness | Easy |

## Question

What is your biggest weakness?

## Answer

Choose something real and non-fatal, then show the mitigation and evidence it is working:

- "I used to go deep on my own before sharing a design. Now I write a one-page design note after a day and ask for review; it cut rework on my last two features."
- Or: "Estimating uncertain AI work. I now timebox spikes and give ranges with explicit assumptions."

Avoid fake weaknesses ("perfectionism") and weaknesses in core requirements ("I don't enjoy testing").

## Likely follow-ups

- How do you know it is improving?
- What feedback have you received recently?
