# B0048 · Building trust with a new team

| Section | Topic | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Behavioural, Role & JPMorganChase | Behavioral - teamwork | Easy |

## Question

How do you build trust when you join a new team?

## Answer

- Listen first: 1:1s with teammates and stakeholders; learn the history before proposing changes.
- Deliver small wins early: fix a bug, improve a doc, take unglamorous tasks.
- Be reliable: do what you say, and warn early if something slips.
- Be transparent: share work in progress and admit mistakes quickly.
- Give credit publicly and help others.

Illustrate with what you did in your last team's first 90 days.

## Likely follow-ups

- What did you change in your first 90 days last time?
- How do you rebuild trust after a mistake?
